## Description
Ginkgo Folio is a quiet reader for the PDFs you already have. Highlight a sentence, ask about it, and the answer appears in the margin next to the text, like a note a good teacher left in the book. There is no chat window to switch to and nothing to copy back.

READ AND MARK
• Highlight and underline in five colours, with Apple Pencil or your finger.
• Write freely on the page, lasso, erase, and undo anything.
• A scratch sheet beside the book for working things out.

ASK IN THE MARGIN
• Translate, explain or ask a question about any passage.
• Follow up on a note in a side column that keeps the whole exchange.
• Reading in another language? Look up a word for a dictionary card, or translate a sentence for its key expressions and an optional grammar walk-through, pitched at your level.
• Notes inside notes: mark a word within a sentence you already translated, and the two cards link to each other.

YOURS, EVERYWHERE
• Your books, marks and notes sync through your own iCloud to your iPad devices.
• Review mode shows every note beside the page; export notes as Markdown or the PDF with standard annotations.

BRING YOUR OWN AI
Ginkgo Folio works with OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini using your own API key, stored only in your device's Keychain. Before anything is sent, the app tells you exactly what goes to which service and asks for your permission. The developer has no server and never sees your books or questions.

Available in English and Simplified Chinese, following your device language. AI answers can be in English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German or Spanish.

## App Review notes
Ginkgo Folio is a PDF reader; AI features use the reader's own API key (bring your own key), entered in Settings (gear icon) › AI service › API key. A test key is provided below with a small spending limit.

1. The library starts empty: tap + to import any PDF from Files, or share a PDF to Ginkgo from another app.
2. Select text and tap Translate, Explain or Ask. The first time, the app asks permission to send text to the chosen AI service; tap Agree and send.
3. The answer appears as a card in the margin. Tap Follow up to continue in the side column.
